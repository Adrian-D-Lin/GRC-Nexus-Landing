MAS NOTICE 626 / FINCEN 31 CFR 1010.410 — CROSS-BORDER WIRE TRANSFERS
REGULATORY AUTHORITY: MONETARY AUTHORITY OF SINGAPORE (MAS)
SCOPE: PAYMENT SERVICES ACT (PS ACT) — MONEY LAUNDERING & TERRORISM FINANCING PREVENTION

§ 13.4 APPLICABILITY TO ELECTRONIC FUND TRANSFERS & CLEARING:
A financial institution effecting or processing a cross-border wire transfer, card network clearance, or stored value remittance of SGD 1,000 or more (or equivalent foreign currency) shall:
(a) Identify and verify the originator and beneficiary identity prior to credit release or clearing execution;
(b) Screen all transaction counterparties against applicable MAS, UN, and OFAC sanctions lists in real time;
(c) Retain full transactional audit records for a mandatory statutory period of not less than 5 years.

§ 13.6 ENHANCED DUE DILIGENCE & POLITICALLY EXPOSED PERSONS (PEPs):
Stored value facility holders, digital wallet providers, and merchant acquirers shall apply enhanced customer due diligence (EDD) where:
(a) A transacting party is identified as a Politically Exposed Person (PEP) or immediate associate;
(b) Rapid mass disbursements or wallet cashouts are executed to unverified foreign banking endpoints.
Automated screening rules must be enforced prior to balance release.

§ 13.9 TRANSACTION MONITORING & VELOCITY CHECKS:
All licensed entities shall establish automated velocity anomaly monitoring on domestic and cross-border currency disbursements to detect structuring, smurfing, or transactions conducted beneath statutory identification limits.
